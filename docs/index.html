        How this works

        Data

        River water levels come from the Department of Irrigation and Drainage (JPS) Public Infobanjir portal. JPS only shows the latest reading, so a GitHub Actions job collects every station every 30 minutes and stores the history in the project repository. Station coordinates come from the data.gov.my flood-warning API.

        Status & estimates

        Status uses each station's own JPS thresholds: Waspada (alert), Amaran (warning) and Bahaya (danger). The trend is a straight line fitted to the last 3 hours of readings, and “time to danger” extends that line to the danger level. That's a simple estimate, not a forecast.

        Data quality

        Readings are flagged rather than hidden: sensors reporting −9999 are offline, readings over 6 hours old are stale, and stations with placeholder or out-of-order thresholds get no status.

        This is an independent student project, not an official warning service. For official flood information, check JPS Public Infobanjir.
